## Campanha

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original fashion campaign photo for the right half of a Brazilian independent clothing store homepage, brand concept Nuvix. Create a sophisticated natural editorial fashion photograph, square format. Editorial magazine photograph, not 3D or illustration.
